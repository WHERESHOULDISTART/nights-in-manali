# Nights in Manali

A short first-person psychological horror game set at a snowed-in trekkers' camp above Old Manali, Himachal Pradesh. Five nights, two endings, about 30–45 minutes.

**▶ Play free in your browser:** https://whereshouldistart.github.io/nights-in-manali/

No download or install needed. It runs in any modern desktop browser (Chrome or Edge recommended). Headphones strongly recommended.

## The story

One year ago Kabir came to Deodar Ridge Camp with his best friend Rohan. Only Kabir came back. Now he's returned, and the first snow has closed the only road down.

Dolma, who runs the camp, tells the old Lahauli story of the **Ulta**, the one with backward feet, whose footprints always point the wrong way. Her daughter Tashi sells something she calls *kaala madhu*, black honey. Ananya, a doctor who stopped practising, is the only one who isn't drinking it.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Walk / run | WASD or arrow keys / hold Shift |
| Look around | Move the mouse (click to lock it) |
| Interact / continue | E, Space or click |
| Pause menu | Esc or P |
| Stop a voice line / voices on-off | V |
| Mute | M |
| Touch | Left side walks, right side looks, tap to interact |

## Features

- Retro dithered first-person look: a Kath-Kuni hut, deodar forest, apple orchard, pagoda temple, and Manali's lights in the valley below.
- Fully voiced characters, with a voice cast menu to choose voices and speaking pace.
- Music that grows tenser every night.
- A monster that moves only when you aren't looking at her.
- Mind-bending quests, a choice that changes later dialogue, and two endings.

**About the voices:** on this free version the characters speak with your browser's built-in voices. Microsoft Edge has the most natural-sounding ones.

## Content note

Drug use and hallucination, death, grief and jump scares. Nothing graphic is shown.

## Credits

Inspired by Himachali and Lahauli mountain folklore and by the indie game *Mouthwashing*. Deodar Ridge Camp and every character are fictional.

The whole game is one self-contained `index.html`: a hand-written raycasting engine with procedural graphics and procedural audio, and no external assets.
